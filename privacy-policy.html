Chakavak Player Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026

Chakavak Player is a player for your own music and video files. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: the app does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data.

Information the app does not collect

  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • No personal information (name, e-mail, phone number, contacts, location) or advertising identifier is collected.
  • No analytics, tracking, crash-reporting or advertising services are included.
  • Nothing about you is sent to the developer or to any third party. The only exceptions are the optional lyrics search and subtitle search described below, which you start yourself.

Your music and video files

  • The app reads the folders you choose with your device's folder picker (on devices without one, from the app's own folder browser), together with the folders inside them, and the music folders it finds on the device (see below).
  • It reads the files and the information inside them (title, artist, album, track number, cover picture) to show and play them. For videos it may take a still picture from the file to show in the list; it stays on the device.
  • When you edit a song's information or an album's cover, the change is written into the files on your device, only when you ask for it. Android may first ask you to allow this.
  • Your library settings (folders, My Favourite, playlists, play order, equalizer, subtitle settings and where each video was left) are saved only on your device.
  • On the first start, and when you ask, the app lists the music folders of your device (from the system media list) and adds them; the list stays on your device.
  • Backup: you can save these settings into a file and restore them later; the file goes only where you save it. Android's own backup (Google) may also keep a copy with your Google account, if you have it turned on in the device settings.

Subtitles and film facts (optional, in the video player)

  • To find subtitles and the film's facts, the app sends only the video's name (taken from its file name, with the year and, for series, the season and episode) to IMDb's public search, which returns the film's IMDb id. With that id it asks OpenSubtitles v3 (opensubtitles-v3.strem.io) for subtitles and Cinemeta (v3-cinemeta.strem.io) for the facts (rating, plot, cast). Nothing that identifies you is sent.
  • The subtitles are looked up when a video opens; the one you pick is saved only on your device. These services' own terms apply.

Lyrics

  • When you tap Find online, or open the lyrics of a song that has none yet, the app asks LRCLIB (lrclib.net), a free public lyrics database, for the song's lyrics. Only the song's title, singer name and length are sent. Nothing that identifies you is sent.
  • Import lyrics reads a lyrics file (.lrc or .txt) that you pick on your device; nothing is sent.
  • Lyrics are saved only on your device. LRCLIB's own terms apply to its service.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show the playback controls while music plays.
  • Foreground service (media playback): so music keeps playing when the app is in the background or the screen is off.
  • Internet: only for the optional lyrics and subtitle searches above and for the store's purchases.
  • Folder access: only for the folders you pick, to read your music and, if you edit song information, to save it into the files.
  • Music and audio: to find the music folders of your device (and, on devices without a folder picker, to show the folders so you can choose one). Nothing leaves the device.
  • Home-screen widget: only shows the play keys and sends your taps to the player.

Purchases

  • Unlocking Pro is handled entirely by the store you installed the app from (Google Play or Cafe Bazaar).
  • The developer has no access to your payment details, such as card numbers.
  • Only the result of the purchase is kept on your device, so that Pro stays active. Google's privacy policy applies to payments: policies.google.com/privacy.

Deleting information

  • Uninstalling the app removes all of its information from the device.
  • Your music files, and any changes you chose to save into them, stay on your device.

Children

  • The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Changes

  • If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
